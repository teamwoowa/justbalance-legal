Delete your Just Balance / JustBalance account and data

App: Just Balance (JustBalance) · Developer: Woowa

Request deletion by email, without the app

  1. Send an email from the address registered to your JustBalance account to team.woowa@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject “JustBalance account and data deletion request” and state that you want your account and its associated data deleted. If you cannot access your registered email, contact the same address for help verifying ownership.
  3. We may ask you to confirm ownership before deletion. Do not send your password or authentication codes. We will reply within 30 days and confirm when deletion is complete.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Tap your email address / Account management.
  3. Choose Delete account, then confirm both the warning and final deletion prompt while online.

Successful in-app deletion removes your account and associated records from the live service database. Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete the account. Deleting JustBalance does not delete your Google account.
